Privacy Policy — Matome Auto Uploader

Last updated: 2026-07-25
Contact: machiappjp@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when authorized operators use Matome Auto Uploader (“the App”) to upload videos to YouTube channels that our organization owns or controls.

1. Who this App is for

The App is an internal tool. It is not offered as a public SaaS to unrelated third parties. Only authorized operators within our organization may use it.

2. Information we access and store

When an operator connects a YouTube channel via Google OAuth 2.0, the App may access and store:

  • Google account identifiers needed for authentication
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens (stored as secrets on secured systems / CI)
  • Video metadata we create (title, description, tags, thumbnails we upload)
  • Video IDs returned by YouTube after upload

We use the YouTube Data API scope limited to upload-related functionality (e.g. youtube.upload).

3. How we use information

We use this information only to authenticate to YouTube on behalf of channels we manage; upload videos, set thumbnails, and manage upload metadata; and operate/troubleshoot the App.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use the API to scrape YouTube at scale or to provide upload access to the general public.

4. Sharing

We do not share OAuth tokens or channel data with unaffiliated third parties, except Google/YouTube as required for API services, and infrastructure providers strictly necessary to run our systems (e.g. GitHub Actions) under confidentiality controls.

5. Google / YouTube data

Use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

6. Revoking access and deletion

Channel owners / Google account holders can revoke the App’s access at any time:

https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

After revocation, operators should delete stored tokens from our secrets storage. To request deletion of residual operational logs, contact machiappjp@gmail.com. We will process requests within a reasonable period unless retention is required by law.

7. Data retention

OAuth tokens are retained only while a channel remains connected for uploading. Tokens are removed when access is revoked or a channel is decommissioned. Application logs may be retained for a limited period for security and debugging.

8. Security

Tokens and credentials are stored in restricted secret stores, not in public repositories. Access is limited to authorized operators.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction).

10. Changes

We may update this Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the App after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
